Z-Score Explainability Framework
Translating spatial distance into physiological domain attributions

Every DBSCAN noise outlier is processed through our Clinical Recommendation & Solution Engine to attribute which specific biomarker deviations caused the outlier status:

Z = (x - μ) / σ • Threshold: |Z| ≥ 1.7 (Significant), |Z| ≥ 3.0 (Critical)

Features exceeding thresholds are assigned to physiological domains (Cardiovascular, Renal, Metabolic, Hematological, Inflammatory) to generate non-prescriptive specialist referrals and confirmatory investigations.
