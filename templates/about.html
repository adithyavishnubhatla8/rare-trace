About RARETRACE

Tracing Hidden Rare-Disease Patterns • Unsupervised Anomaly Detection Platform • B.Tech Academic Capstone

Project Abstract & Clinical Motivation

RARETRACE addresses the persistent healthcare bottleneck where rare clinical conditions remain unidentified due to the extreme scarcity of labeled training exemplars. In the absence of ground-truth diagnostic labels, conventional supervised classifiers fail.

By employing Density-Based Spatial Clustering of Applications with Noise (DBSCAN) coupled with an Isolation Forest Benchmark on standardized multidimensional biomarker arrays, RARETRACE naturally groups normative cohort majorities into dense topological clusters while isolating low-density outliers as Candidate Rare-Disease Cases (Noise: -1).

MEDICAL DISCLAIMER: RARETRACE is an academic machine-learning research prototype for identifying statistical anomalies in datasets. It does not provide medical diagnosis or clinical advice. DBSCAN anomalies indicate statistical noise points and must not be interpreted as confirmed clinical diagnoses.
Core Analytical Architecture
1. Schema-Agnostic Ingestion: Dynamic patient CSV parsing, type inference, and relational isolation in PostgreSQL / SQLite.
2. Unsupervised DBSCAN: Density connectivity parameterization via automated K-distance elbow curve detection.
3. Z-Score Explainability: Attribution of patient outlier status to specific organ systems (|Z| ≥ 1.7 and |Z| ≥ 3.0).
4. Recommendation Engine: 4-tier clinical priority stratification and 6-category structured decision-support dossiers.
