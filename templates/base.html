RARETRACE Clinical Intelligence Guide

1. How does Anomaly Detection work?
RARETRACE runs unsupervised DBSCAN clustering on standardized patient biomarker arrays. Patients falling into low-density sparse regions (noise label -1) are flagged as candidate rare-disease cases.

2. What is the Anomaly Score?
Calculated using Euclidean k-NN distance in multi-dimensional standardized space, scaled from 0 to 100.

3. How are recommendations generated?
The Clinical Recommendation Engine computes feature-level Z-scores relative to the normative cohort mean, matching identified physiological domain deviations (Cardiovascular, Renal, Metabolic, Hematological) to clinical guidelines.
